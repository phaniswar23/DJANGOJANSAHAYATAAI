Easy Process

How It Works

A simple 4-step process from reporting a problem to getting it fixed.

Step 01

1. Report the Problem

Describe what happened. Add photos, an audio note, or your location.

Step 02

2. Sent to the Right Office

We route your complaint straight to the responsible local office.

Step 03

3. Field Team Fixes It

The field crew carries out the repair and uploads photos as proof.

Step 04

4. You Check & Sign Off

You inspect the proof photos. If satisfied, close it. If not, reopen it.
