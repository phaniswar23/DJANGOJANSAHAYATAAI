JanSahayata AI routes your issue directly to local field officers without middlemen.

Citizen Legal Rights

Your Guaranteed Rights as a Citizen

Right to Photo Proof

No officer or department can mark your issue "Resolved" without uploading genuine photographic proof of the finished work.

Right to Dispute

If an issue is marked resolved falsely, you have the statutory right to 1-click dispute. It immediately triggers an executive inquiry.

Live SLA Countdown

Every complaint has an official deadline. If breached, the system automatically alerts the Department Head and Super Administrator.

Zero Cost & Anti-Bribe

100% free government redressal. Direct automated digital routing guarantees zero bribery or harassment by middlemen.
