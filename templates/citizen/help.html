Department Service Level Standards (SLAs)

Electricity (DISCOM):

Power interruptions resolved within 4–12 hours. High-voltage shock hazards secured under 4 hours.

Water Supply & Sewerage:

Contamination and burst pipelines attended within 12–24 hours.

Roads & PWD:

Critical road cave-ins barricaded under 6 hours; permanent pothole repair within 48–72 hours.

Railways (RailMadad):

Running coach sanitation attended at next major station; infrastructure repairs within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I file a complaint without creating an account?

Yes! You can lodge a public grievance directly from the "File a Complaint" page. A reference number will be generated immediately for tracking.

Q: What should I do if an officer marks my problem resolved without fixing it?

Simply open your complaint details using the reference number and click "Reopen / File Dispute". This triggers an executive escalations workflow.
